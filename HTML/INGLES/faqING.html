Frequently Asked Questions

This section aims to assist users who have unanswered questions about cancer. We recognize that while many users seek information on the subject, that information does not always fully address their concerns. Therefore, the Canrisk team seeks to provide answers regarding this disease.


In this section, we provide access to our site's most frequently asked questions. We also offer the option to submit specific questions via a dedicated form; these will be answered within 1 to 7 days via Gmail after the required information is provided.

Complete the Google Forms Questionnaire →
What is cancer and how does it affect the body?

Cancer is a disease caused by the uncontrolled multiplication of abnormal cells in the body; these cells can invade nearby tissues and spread to other parts of the organism.

How can I submit a question that isn't listed here?

You can visit the contact section or fill out the interactive questionnaire above to send us your specific questions. Our medical team will respond to your email within 1 to 7 business days.

Do the answers provided replace a medical consultation?

No. All information provided by Canrisk is for guidance and informational purposes only. If you experience symptoms or a medical emergency, you should immediately see a healthcare professional or call 911.

What should I do if I suspect I have symptoms?

We recommend visiting the nearest health center or hospital for a proper physical examination and the appropriate clinical tests.
